**[Innovation Sandbox for AI](https://www.zh.ch/de/wirtschaft-arbeit/wirtschaftsstandort/innovation-sandbox.html)** – a real-world testing environment for responsible AI innovation in Switzerland.

The [Innovation Sandbox for AI](https://www.zh.ch/en/wirtschaft-arbeit/wirtschaftsstandort/innovation-sandbox.html) was launched in 2022 by the [Office for Economy of the Canton of Zurich](https://www.zh.ch/en/volkswirtschaftsdirektion/amt-fuer-wirtschaft.html), with support from the [Metropolitan Area Zurich initiative](https://www.metropolitanraum-zuerich.ch/). The programme was created **to strengthen the AI ecosystem by enabling practical real-world testing, early regulatory learning, and knowledge transfer between startups, academia, industry, and public authorities**.

Learn more about our work in [our programme report](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/wirtschaft-arbeit/wirtschaftsstandort/dokumente/sandbox_en.pdf).

# Open Source Assets

The Innovation Sandbox for AI promotes **practical knowledge transfer beyond individual pilot projects**. Selected **project outcomes are shared as open-source assets to support responsible AI adoption across sectors**.

Current assets include:

- [AI-assisted precheck prototype for building permits](https://github.com/Byte-Studio-CH/ki-baubewilligung-oss) – Open-source prototype for the automated pre-assessment of selected building permit applications.
- [Visual inspection datasets for infrastructure monitoring and computer vision research](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ibm-research/cif-dataset) – Image datasets collected for infrastructure inspection and AI model development.
- [Real-time smart parking data and API integration from the City of Frauenfeld](https://modi.trustrelay.io/login) – Available through the MODI Sandbox platform *(registration required)*.

The goal is to **enable reuse, transparency, and collaborative learning for startups, researchers, public institutions, and the broader AI ecosystem**.
